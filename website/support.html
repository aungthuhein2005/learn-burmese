Common questions

A letter I wrote correctly got few stars

Write the letter big, in one go, and fill most of the box. If it still feels too strict, a grown-up can open Parents' Corner (lock icon, top right) and set Checking to Easy.

I can't hear the letter sounds

Turn up the volume and check that sounds are on in Parents' Corner. Sounds play even when the Ring/Silent switch is on silent.

The first launch shows a loading bar

On first launch, Akkhara prepares its letters on the device. This takes a few seconds and only happens once.

How do I open Parents' Corner?

Tap the lock icon at the top right of the home screen and answer the multiplication question.

How do I start over?

Parents' Corner › Reset all progress. This clears stars, stickers and the streak.

Does it need the internet?

No. Akkhara works fully offline and never uploads drawings. See the privacy policy.
