Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Englishမြန်မာ
In short: Akkhara does not collect any data. Drawings, stars and settings stay on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Progress (stars per letter, stickers, practice days) and settings (difficulty, sounds, tracing guide) are saved only inside the app on your device.
  • They may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup, if you have backups turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.
  • "Reset all progress" in Parents' Corner erases progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Handwriting checking

When your child taps Check, the drawing is scored by a small model that runs on the iPhone or iPad. Drawings are never saved, uploaded or sent anywhere, and are discarded after scoring.

Internet use

Akkhara does not use the internet. Everything it needs, including the handwriting model and the letter sounds, is included in the app.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No in-app purchases and no links to outside websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data. We have no data to share.

Children

Akkhara is made for children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and it complies with the Apple App Store rules for apps in the Kids category.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
