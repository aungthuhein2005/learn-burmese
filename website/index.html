Akkhara

Learn to write the Myanmar alphabet, one letter at a time.

မြန်မာ အက္ခရာ ၃၃ လုံးကို ရေးကြည့်ပြီး သင်ယူကြမယ်

Akkhara (အက္ခရာ, "alphabet") is an iPhone and iPad app that helps children and beginners learn to write the 33 Myanmar consonants. Hear a letter, draw it with a finger or Apple Pencil, and get up to three stars.

What's inside

  • Learn: hear every letter, recorded by a native speaker
  • Practice: write over a faint guide and get an instant star score
  • Find the Letter: hear a letter and tap it, for children who can't write yet
  • Quiz: write letters from memory; hard letters come back more often
  • Stickers and streaks: a reason to come back every day

Private by design

Handwriting is checked on the device. No internet, no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
